Neurosymbolic AI

Håkan Karlsson Faronius

My research focuses on the mathematical foundations of neurosymbolic AI. I study the interaction between learning and reasoning using probabilistic circuits and Bayesian network representations, and investigate reasoning shortcuts in neurosymbolic systems.

I am also interested in uncertainty estimation in AI and the study of emergence in complex systems.

  • Probabilistic circuits
  • Bayesian networks
  • Reasoning shortcuts
